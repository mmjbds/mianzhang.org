A successful humanoid robot demo is not a deployment contract.

Artifact: observer_depth_profile_v1. This profile separates one completed action from a deployable operating envelope. Its minimum loss account is mission_loss = wrong_action_cost + recovery_cost + unsafe_exposure; task completion alone does not close the review.

Evidence Map Registries Boundaries Counterexamples

Scope

One manipulation result is evidence for one operating condition.

A folding, carrying, or walking demonstration can establish that an action occurred under a recorded setup. It does not by itself establish household reliability, safe interaction, broad object coverage, or unattended operation. The profile keeps the scenario and evidence date visible.

Loss

Completion rate does not price the cost of being wrong.

The review records mission_loss = wrong_action_cost + recovery_cost + unsafe_exposure. A system with a high completion rate can remain expensive or unsafe when failures are hard to detect, recovery is slow, or people and property remain exposed during the error.

Handover

A deployable boundary names both the fallback and the operator.

A useful review states when the robot stops, how it enters a lower-risk state, who can override it, and which scenarios remain untested. This profile does not turn an exhibition result, production forecast, or isolated action into a safety certification or commercial-return guarantee.

The trigger, channel, and accountable operator for taking control from the system.

The tested operating region and the conditions that remain outside the evidence.
