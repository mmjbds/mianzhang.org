System Boundary

Public checks expose interfaces, not production control logic.

The public layer includes scenarios, schemas, fixed examples, validators, aggregate results, claim limits and issue routes. It does not publish customer data, private orchestration, production thresholds, policy weights, deployment credentials or non-public research.

A successful public check shows that one artifact behaves as specified under the released conditions. It is not a safety certification, deployment authorization or claim of general model reliability.
