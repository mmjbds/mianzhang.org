Public website notice

Privacy and website analytics

This public research site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits, referral sources, page performance, and which public routes readers actually use.

Analytics

What is measured

Cloudflare Web Analytics may process technical and aggregate visit information needed to report page views, referral sources, approximate geography, device or browser categories, and web performance. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first analytics that does not collect or use visitors' personal data.

The analytics signal is used to compare public entry pages and improve navigation, performance, and publication decisions. It is not treated as proof that a paper was read, code was run, a claim was accepted, or commercial demand exists.

Site boundary

What these pages do not ask for

  • The public pages do not ask visitors to create an account.
  • The site does not operate a customer database, payment flow, or private submission form.
  • Interactive public demos run with synthetic examples and do not authorize external actions.
  • Third-party destinations such as GitHub, Hugging Face, Zenodo, and email providers apply their own privacy terms after a visitor follows a link.
